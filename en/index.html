FAQ

What is Allong?

Allong is a free iPhone game. Walk together with a small creature hatched from an egg.

Is Allong free?

Yes. It is free to play with no in-app purchases.

How are steps counted?

Steps are counted from the distance you actually move. You can keep walking together with the app closed.

Who can play?

Ages 14 and up.

Do I need an account?

No. You start as a guest, and linking an account lets you restore your Allong on a new device.
